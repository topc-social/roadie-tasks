# Roadie tasks

Tasks and the Neki ledger for Roadie: a dashcam whose footage goes straight to your own cloud, where AI finds traffic violations worth reporting. From an [idea on Ideato](https://www.ideato.social/idea/f1eed218-a351-44e1-9379-8932c00984a6).

- [Issues](../../issues): tasks. Each issue is one task, and its `neki:N` label is the Neki it earns (1 Neki = 1 rupee of work at market price).
- [`LEDGER.md`](LEDGER.md): who has earned what.

Closing a task as completed (by hand or with a merged PR) adds the `accepted` label and its two ledger rows (1% idea, 99% work).
